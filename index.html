Protection
What protects you, if we miss

No promises I can't keep. Here's what's in writing.

Risk 01 · Financial

One-year cliff, then monthly.

Cross month 12 and 0.5% vests instantly. The rest vests monthly across years 2–4. Part ways at month 13 — you keep half a point. Forever.

Risk 02 · Time

Your work is always yours.

Every frame you shoot at Vyzee is yours to use — credited, exportable, no NDA bs. If you leave, you leave with your body of work stays with you.

Risk 03 · Authority

The role is documented.

Title, decision rights, budget authority, hiring power, reporting line is written into the agreement. Not hula-hula shi. Not "we'll figure it out."

Risk 04 · Trust

Clean exit, any time.

30-day notice either way, no claw-back on vested equity, no non-compete that boxes you out of creative work in Manila. You owe us your best, not your future.

My pledge to you — on the record

"I would rather lose you to something better than keep you in something that isn't worth it." — Shadiboy

Questions
The questions you haven't asked yet

Four questions in the back of your head.

Q · 01
"What if Vyzee doesn't make it past year one?"
You keep your half-point at the cliff, your portfolio comes with you, and you go back to freelancing with a Creative Director title and twelve months of real agency experience and you will not lose.
Q · 02
"Can I still take side gigs?"
Yes — weddings, personal art projects, friends' brands. The only restriction: no other agency targeting the same services around the Metro. That's it.
Q · 03
"What if I burn out doing two shoots a week?"
You hire the junior shooter at month four. By month eight you're directing more than shooting. The role gets lighter on cam from time to time.
Q · 04
"WHY ME AND NOT SOMEONE CHEAPER?"
Because we don't want a random. We want a someone that builds our visual voice of a company we're building for ten years. Cheaper hires fill seats fast while you build a function. I'd rather pay you in equity than lose you in three months.
If the question keeping you up isn't here — ask it before slide thirteen.
